## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Allergen Season

When shopping for a robot vacuum specifically to combat spring allergens, focus on these critical features that separate mediocre cleaners from allergen-fighting powerhouses.

**HEPA filtration is non-negotiable.** True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns and larger, which includes most pollen grains (typically 10-100 microns) and fine dust. Skip any vacuum that claims "HEPA-style" or "HEPA-type" filtration — these marketing terms don't meet actual HEPA standards and won't provide the allergen protection you need.

**Suction power matters more during allergen season.** Look for vacuums with at least 2,000 Pa of suction, though 2,500+ Pa is ideal for lifting embedded pollen from carpet fibers. Many budget models top out around 1,400 Pa, which simply won't cut through the thick layer of spring allergens that accumulate on floors and furniture.

**Self-emptying capability becomes crucial** when dealing with allergen-heavy debris. Manual emptying exposes you to concentrated allergens every few days, potentially triggering symptoms. Self-emptying bases with sealed bags or bagless systems keep collected particles contained for weeks at a time.

**Multi-floor mapping and scheduling** allow you to target high-allergen areas more frequently. Entryways, bedrooms, and living rooms typically need daily cleaning during peak pollen season, while storage areas might only need weekly attention.
