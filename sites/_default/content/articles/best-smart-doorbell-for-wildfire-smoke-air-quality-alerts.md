## What to Look For in a Smart Doorbell for Air Quality Monitoring

When shopping for a smart doorbell that can handle wildfire smoke detection, **sensor sensitivity and accuracy** should be your top priority. Look for devices with particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10) sensors rated for detection levels below 35 μg/m³ — the threshold where air quality becomes unhealthy for sensitive groups. The best models can detect particles as small as 0.3 microns, which covers the fine particulates that make wildfire smoke so dangerous.

**Real-time alert customization** is equally critical. You need a doorbell that can distinguish between normal outdoor activity and actual air quality threats, with the ability to set custom thresholds based on your local air quality baseline. The most effective systems allow you to create different alert profiles for various AQI levels, sending immediate notifications when readings spike above your preset limits.

**Integration with broader smart home ecosystems** makes the difference between a standalone warning system and a comprehensive protection strategy. Look for doorbells that work seamlessly with smart HVAC systems, air purifiers, and home automation platforms like SmartThings or Hubitat, allowing automatic responses like closing smart vents or activating air filtration when smoke is detected.

**Battery life during extended monitoring periods** becomes crucial during wildfire season when continuous air quality monitoring can drain power faster than normal operation. The best models maintain at least 3-4 months of battery life even with enhanced environmental monitoring running 24/7.
