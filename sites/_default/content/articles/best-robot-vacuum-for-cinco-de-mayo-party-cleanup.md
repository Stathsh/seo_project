## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Party Cleanup

When you're dealing with post-party messes, your robot vacuum needs to handle more than just daily dust bunnies. **Strong suction power** is non-negotiable — you'll be dealing with everything from crushed tortilla chips to spilled salsa remnants, which means you need at least 2,000 Pa of suction to effectively lift stubborn debris from both hard floors and carpets.

**Advanced navigation and obstacle avoidance** becomes crucial when your floors are littered with party supplies, dropped food, and potentially wet spots from spilled drinks. Look for models with camera-based navigation or advanced sensors that can identify and navigate around obstacles without getting stuck or spreading messes around.

**Large dustbin capacity or self-emptying capability** is essential because party cleanup generates significantly more debris than regular daily cleaning. Standard 0.3-liter dustbins will fill up quickly with chips, confetti, and other party remnants, so aim for at least 0.4 liters or invest in a self-emptying model.

**Multi-surface cleaning ability** matters since Cinco de Mayo parties typically span kitchens, dining rooms, and outdoor patios. Your robot needs to transition seamlessly between tile, hardwood, rugs, and potentially handle slightly damp areas where drinks were spilled.
