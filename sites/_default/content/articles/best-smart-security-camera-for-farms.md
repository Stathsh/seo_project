## What to Look For in Farm Security Cameras

When securing agricultural property, your camera needs differ dramatically from typical home security setups. **Cellular connectivity** is non-negotiable since most farms lack reliable WiFi coverage across fields and outbuildings. Look for cameras with 4G LTE capability and compatibility with major carriers like Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile.

**Weather resistance** goes beyond basic IP65 ratings. Farm cameras face extreme temperature swings, driving rain, dust, and UV exposure for years. Seek cameras rated for -4°F to 140°F operation with robust weatherproofing.

**Power independence** matters immensely when monitoring remote locations. Solar panels with substantial battery backup (minimum 15,000mAh) ensure continuous operation during cloudy periods. Some cameras offer 3-6 months of battery life on a single charge.

**Coverage area and night vision** determine your camera's effectiveness. Pan-tilt functionality covers more ground than fixed cameras, while infrared range of 100+ feet illuminates distant threats. Motion detection zones help filter livestock movement from genuine security concerns.
