## What to Look For in AI Person Detection Cameras

**Detection Accuracy and False Alert Reduction** matters most when choosing an AI security camera. Look for cameras that can distinguish between people, vehicles, animals, and general motion with at least 90% accuracy. The best models use edge AI processing (on-device) rather than cloud-based detection, which means faster alerts and no dependency on internet connectivity. Cameras with customizable detection zones and sensitivity settings give you more control over what triggers notifications.

**Video Quality and Night Vision Capabilities** directly impact AI performance since algorithms need clear imagery to work effectively. Prioritize cameras with at least 2K resolution (1440p), though 4K is becoming the sweet spot for detailed person identification. Color night vision with built-in spotlights significantly improves nighttime AI accuracy compared to traditional infrared, allowing the camera to capture facial features and clothing details that help with identification.

**Power Options and Storage Flexibility** determine where you can install your camera and ongoing costs. Solar-powered cameras with battery backup offer the most installation flexibility, while hardwired options provide consistent power for continuous recording. For storage, look for cameras supporting both local storage (microSD cards) and cloud options. Avoid cameras that require cloud subscriptions for AI features — the best models process AI detection locally and only use cloud storage as an optional backup.

**Smart Home Integration and Alert Customization** enhance the overall security experience. Cameras compatible with Alexa, Google Home, or Apple HomeKit allow voice control and integration with other smart devices. Advanced models offer customizable alert types (person vs. vehicle), scheduling options to disable alerts during certain hours, and two-way audio for interaction with visitors or deterring intruders.
