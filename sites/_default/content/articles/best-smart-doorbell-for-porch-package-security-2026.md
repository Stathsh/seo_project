## What to Look For in a Package Security Doorbell

**Video Quality and Coverage Area:** You need at least 1080p resolution with a wide field of view (130+ degrees) to clearly see package details and capture faces. Look for HDR support to handle harsh sunlight and shadows on your porch. The camera should cover your entire package drop zone, not just the immediate doorway.

**Advanced Motion Detection:** Generic motion alerts are useless for package security. You want AI-powered detection that can differentiate between delivery trucks, people approaching with packages, and random motion like falling leaves. Package-specific alerts and person detection are game-changers for staying on top of deliveries.

**Two-Way Audio Quality:** Clear audio is crucial for giving delivery instructions ("please place behind the planter") or deterring package thieves. Look for noise cancellation and adjustable volume levels so you can communicate effectively even during busy street hours.

**Cloud Storage and Local Options:** Reliable video storage is non-negotiable. Consider both monthly subscription costs and local storage options. Some doorbells offer free basic cloud storage, while others require paid plans for essential features like person detection.
