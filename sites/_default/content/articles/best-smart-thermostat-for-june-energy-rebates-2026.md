## What to Look For in a Smart Thermostat for June Energy Rebates

**ENERGY STAR Certification** is non-negotiable for June Energy's 2026 rebate program. Only certified models qualify for the full rebate amounts, which range from $75 to $150 depending on your utility zone. Look for the blue ENERGY STAR label and verify the model number on June Energy's approved device list.

**Advanced Scheduling and Learning Features** directly impact your rebate eligibility and savings potential. June Energy requires thermostats to demonstrate at least 15% energy savings compared to manual thermostats. Models with occupancy sensing, geofencing, and adaptive learning algorithms consistently hit or exceed this threshold.

**Smart Grid Integration** is increasingly important as June Energy rolls out demand response programs. Thermostats with built-in smart grid connectivity can automatically adjust during peak hours, earning you additional incentive payments beyond the initial rebate.

**Professional Installation Requirements** vary by rebate tier. While some qualify for DIY installation rebates, the highest rebate amounts ($125-$150) require certified HVAC professional installation with documentation. Factor this $150-250 installation cost into your total investment calculation.
