## What to Look For in a Smart Thermostat for Manufactured Homes

### Wi-Fi Reliability and Range
Manufactured homes often have weaker Wi-Fi signals due to metal construction and longer distances from routers. Look for thermostats with dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4GHz and 5GHz) and strong signal reception. Models with built-in Wi-Fi repeaters or mesh capabilities are even better.

### C-Wire Flexibility
Many manufactured homes lack a common wire (C-wire) for continuous power to smart thermostats. Choose models that include power extender kits or can operate reliably without a C-wire. This flexibility prevents costly rewiring in homes where accessing ductwork is challenging.

### Remote Sensor Compatibility
Manufactured homes frequently have hot and cold spots due to their layout and insulation challenges. Thermostats that support remote sensors let you monitor and average temperatures across multiple zones, ensuring consistent comfort throughout your home.

### Compact Installation Requirements
Space around thermostats in manufactured homes is often limited by narrow hallways and lower ceilings. Look for models with smaller displays and minimal wire management requirements that won't create installation headaches in tight spaces.
