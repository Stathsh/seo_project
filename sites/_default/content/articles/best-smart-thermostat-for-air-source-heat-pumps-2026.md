## What to Look For in a Smart Thermostat for Heat Pumps

**Heat Pump-Specific Programming** is non-negotiable. Heat pumps work differently than gas furnaces — they're most efficient when maintaining steady temperatures rather than making large temperature swings. Look for thermostats with dedicated heat pump modes that minimize the use of auxiliary/emergency heat, which can cost 3-4x more to operate than the heat pump itself.

**Multi-Stage Compatibility** matters more with heat pumps than any other system type. Your thermostat needs to properly control both the heat pump and backup heating elements, typically supporting at least 2-heat/1-cool staging. Premium models offer up to 4-heat/2-cool staging for variable-capacity systems.

**Advanced Algorithms and Learning** become crucial with heat pumps because of their slower response times. The best thermostats learn your heat pump's characteristics and pre-condition your home, starting the heating process earlier to reach your target temperature at the right time without triggering expensive auxiliary heat.

**Room Sensors and Zoning** address heat pumps' tendency toward uneven heating. Since heat pumps move air differently than forced-air systems, having multiple temperature sensors helps the thermostat make better decisions about when and how long to run the system.
