## What to Look For in a Heat-Resistant Smart Doorbell

**Operating Temperature Range** is the most critical spec — look for doorbells rated for at least 120°F, though 140°F is ideal for desert climates. Many budget models fail around 100°F, leaving you without security when you need it most.

**Battery Chemistry and Management** determines whether your doorbell survives summer. Lithium-ion batteries degrade rapidly in heat, while some manufacturers use heat-resistant battery formulations or advanced thermal management systems that prevent overheating damage.

**Heat Dissipation Design** separates quality units from cheap alternatives. Look for metal housings, thermal vents, or active cooling features. Plastic-bodied doorbells often warp or fail in direct sunlight, especially on south-facing doors.

**Cloud Storage Reliability** becomes crucial when local storage fails. Heat can corrupt SD cards and internal storage, so prioritize models with robust cloud backup systems that won't lose your security footage during temperature extremes.
