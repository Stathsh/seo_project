## What to Look For in a Smart Doorbell for Delivery Drivers

When choosing a smart doorbell specifically for delivery work, four factors matter more than fancy features you'll never use.

**Video quality and field of view** top the list — you need to clearly see package placement, faces, and vehicle details. Look for at least 1080p resolution with a 160-degree horizontal field of view. Anything less and you'll miss crucial details when reviewing footage.

**Motion detection speed and accuracy** can make or break your delivery documentation. The best doorbells trigger recording within 1-2 seconds of movement, not the sluggish 5-6 seconds some budget models require. Advanced motion zones let you focus on delivery areas while ignoring street traffic.

**Battery life versus hardwired reliability** becomes critical when you're logging 8-12 hour shifts. Battery models offer installation flexibility but typically last 2-3 months with heavy use. Hardwired doorbells provide consistent power but require existing doorbell wiring or professional installation.

**Mobile app responsiveness** during delivery rushes separates good doorbells from great ones. The app should load instantly, offer quick footage scrubbing, and provide reliable push notifications. Laggy apps waste precious time between stops.
