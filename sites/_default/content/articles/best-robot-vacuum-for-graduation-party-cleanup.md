## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Party Cleanup

### Obstacle Avoidance Technology
Post-party floors are chaos. Between dropped graduation caps, scattered confetti, stray gift bags, and forgotten phone chargers, you need a robot vacuum with advanced obstacle detection. Look for models with camera-based navigation or LiDAR mapping that can identify and navigate around objects rather than pushing them around or getting stuck. The last thing you want is your vacuum dragging streamers across the room or getting tangled in balloon strings.

### Large Dustbin Capacity
Party cleanup generates significantly more debris than daily maintenance cleaning. Standard robot vacuum dustbins (typically 0.3-0.6 liters) will fill up quickly with confetti, food crumbs, and general party debris. Models with larger dustbins (0.7+ liters) or automatic emptying stations can handle the volume without requiring multiple manual interventions during cleanup.

### Strong Suction Power
Party messes aren't just surface-level dust. You're dealing with ground-in cake crumbs, scattered popcorn, and possibly sticky spills that have dried. Look for vacuums with at least 2000 Pa of suction power, though 2500+ Pa is ideal for tackling the mix of fine confetti and larger debris that graduation parties generate.

### Multi-Surface Cleaning
Graduation parties rarely stay in one room or on one surface type. Your vacuum needs to seamlessly transition between hardwood, carpet, and area rugs while maintaining consistent cleaning performance. Models with automatic carpet detection that boost suction on rugs are particularly valuable for thorough party cleanup.
