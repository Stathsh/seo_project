## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Allergens

**HEPA Filtration is Non-Negotiable**
True HEPA filters capture particles down to 0.3 microns with 99.97% efficiency, which includes dust mites (10-40 microns), pollen (10-200 microns), and pet dander (0.5-100 microns). Avoid vacuums with "HEPA-style" or "HEPA-type" filters — these are marketing terms that don't meet the strict HEPA standard.

**Suction Power Above 2000Pa**
Dust mites burrow deep into carpet fibers and upholstery. You need at least 2000Pa of suction to dislodge them effectively. Lower-powered vacuums might pick up surface debris but leave the allergen-producing mites behind.

**Sealed System Design**
A vacuum can have the best HEPA filter in the world, but if air leaks around it, you're just redistributing allergens. Look for models with fully sealed air paths that force all air through the filtration system.

**Multi-Surface Cleaning with Carpet Boost**
Dust mites primarily live in carpets and rugs, but they also inhabit hard surfaces where skin cells accumulate. The best allergen-fighting robots automatically increase suction on carpets while maintaining effective cleaning on hard floors.
