## What to Look For in a HomeKit Smart Garage Door Opener

**Native HomeKit Integration vs. Bridge Requirements:** Some openers require additional hardware bridges to work with HomeKit, adding complexity and potential failure points. Look for models with built-in HomeKit support that connect directly to your network without intermediary devices.

**Wi-Fi Connectivity Standards:** Dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4GHz and 5GHz) support ensures reliable connectivity even in crowded wireless environments. Single-band openers often struggle with interference from other smart home devices, leading to delayed responses or connection drops.

**Motor Power and Compatibility:** Match the motor specifications to your garage door weight and type. Chain-drive models typically handle heavier doors (up to 550 lbs) while belt-drive systems offer quieter operation but usually max out around 400 lbs. Verify compatibility with your existing door hardware before purchasing.

**Security Features:** End-to-end encryption is non-negotiable for garage access. Look for rolling code technology that changes the access code with each use, and two-way communication that confirms when commands are received and executed.
