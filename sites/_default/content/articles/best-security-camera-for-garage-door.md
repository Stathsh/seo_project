## What to Look For in a Garage Door Security Camera

When shopping for garage door security, prioritize **motion detection accuracy** over basic motion sensing. Generic cameras trigger constantly from car headlights, shadows, and garage door movement itself. Look for AI-powered detection that differentiates between vehicles, people, and pets — this feature alone will save you from hundreds of false notifications.

**Night vision quality** matters more for garages than typical outdoor areas. Garage interiors create challenging lighting with deep shadows and harsh LED bulb glare. Seek cameras with color night vision or infrared ranges exceeding 30 feet, plus adjustable sensitivity settings to handle your garage's specific lighting quirks.

**Power flexibility** is crucial since garage electrical access varies wildly. Battery-powered cameras offer the easiest installation but require regular charging or solar panel additions. Wired options provide reliability but demand professional installation in many garages. Consider your garage's layout and your comfort level with running cables.

**Storage options** significantly impact long-term costs. Cloud subscriptions for garage cameras add up quickly since they trigger frequently. Local storage via microSD cards or network video recorders (NVRs) eliminates monthly fees while providing faster access to footage when you need it most.
