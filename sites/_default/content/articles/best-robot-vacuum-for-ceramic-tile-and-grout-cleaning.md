## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Ceramic Tile

### Dual-Action Cleaning System
The most effective tile cleaners combine strong suction (at least 3,000 Pa) with active mopping. Look for models with vibrating or rotating mop pads rather than simple drag-along cloths. The vibration helps agitate dirt from grout lines that suction alone can't reach.

### Advanced Navigation for Grout Coverage
LiDAR navigation systems create precise maps that ensure the vacuum travels in straight lines across your tiles, maximizing grout line coverage. Avoid basic bump-and-turn models that create random patterns and miss grout intersections.

### Adjustable Water Flow Control
Ceramic tiles can handle moisture, but you want control over how much water hits your floors. Look for models with multiple water flow settings and smart sensors that can adjust based on floor type and dirt levels.

### Self-Maintenance Features
Tile cleaning generates more debris than carpet cleaning. The best models include self-emptying bases, automatic mop washing, and heated drying to prevent bacterial growth on cleaning pads.
