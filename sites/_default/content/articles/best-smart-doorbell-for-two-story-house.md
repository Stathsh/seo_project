## What to Look For in a Smart Doorbell for Two-Story Houses

### Video Quality and Field of View
Your doorbell needs exceptional video resolution and a wide viewing angle since you'll often be monitoring from a distance. Look for at least 1080p resolution, though 1536p or 2K is better for identifying faces and reading package labels from your upstairs windows. A field of view between 160-180 degrees ensures you can see visitors approaching from the sides and packages left on your porch.

### Wireless Range and Connectivity
Two-story homes can strain Wi-Fi connections, especially if your router is on a different floor than your doorbell. Choose doorbells with strong wireless radios and consider models that include Wi-Fi extenders or work with mesh networks. Battery-powered options eliminate wiring concerns but need reliable connectivity to function properly.

### Audio Quality and Volume
Standard doorbell chimes might not reach upper floors effectively. Look for doorbells with loud, clear two-way audio and the ability to connect multiple chimes throughout your home. Some models offer customizable chime volumes and smartphone notifications that ensure you're always alerted.

### Smart Home Integration
Multi-story homes benefit from comprehensive smart home integration. Choose doorbells that work with your existing ecosystem — whether that's Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit — so you can receive alerts on smart displays, speakers, and devices throughout your house.
