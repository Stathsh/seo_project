## What to Look For in Smart Plant Grow Lights

### Full-Spectrum LED Coverage
Your plants need more than just bright light — they need the right wavelengths. Look for lights offering both blue spectrum (400-500nm) for vegetative growth and red spectrum (600-700nm) for flowering and fruiting. The best smart grow lights provide PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density) measurements of at least 100-200 μmol/m²/s at typical hanging distances for most houseplants.

### Smart Controls and Scheduling
The "smart" in smart grow lights should include comprehensive app control with scheduling capabilities. Premium models offer sunrise/sunset simulation, different intensity levels throughout the day, and the ability to create custom programs for different plant types. Timer precision matters — look for lights that can adjust in 15-minute increments or smaller.

### Heat Management and Energy Efficiency
LED efficiency is measured in μmol/J (micromoles per joule), with quality grow lights achieving 2.0+ μmol/J. Poor heat management can damage both your plants and the light fixture itself. Look for lights with aluminum heat sinks, proper ventilation design, and thermal protection features.

### Coverage Area and Mounting Flexibility
Consider your growing space carefully. Strip lights work well for shelving setups and long planters, while panel lights provide better coverage for multiple plants. Most manufacturers provide coverage specifications, but remember that light intensity decreases significantly with distance — doubling the distance quarters the light intensity.
