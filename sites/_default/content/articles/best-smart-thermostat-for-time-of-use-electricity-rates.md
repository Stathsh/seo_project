## What to Look For in a Smart Thermostat for Time-of-Use Rates

When choosing a thermostat for time-of-use (TOU) electricity pricing, you need features that go beyond basic programming. **Advanced scheduling capabilities** are non-negotiable — look for models that offer multiple daily schedules and can automatically adjust based on your utility's specific rate structure. The best options let you set different temperatures for peak, off-peak, and super off-peak periods.

**Learning algorithms and occupancy detection** make a massive difference in TOU scenarios. Smart thermostats that can detect when you're home and adjust accordingly prevent you from heating or cooling an empty house during expensive peak hours. Room sensors that monitor multiple zones help ensure comfort while minimizing energy use.

**Energy usage reporting and integration capabilities** are crucial for monitoring your savings. Choose thermostats that provide detailed energy reports showing exactly how much you're spending during different rate periods. Models that integrate with utility programs or smart home ecosystems can automatically respond to demand response events for additional savings.

**Pre-cooling and thermal mass strategies** separate basic programmable thermostats from truly smart ones. The best TOU thermostats can pre-cool your home during cheaper morning hours, then reduce cooling during expensive afternoon peak periods while maintaining comfort through thermal mass.
