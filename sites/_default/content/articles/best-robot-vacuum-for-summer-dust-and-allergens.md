## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Summer Dust and Allergens

When shopping for a robot vacuum to combat summer's dust and pollen invasion, four features separate the winners from the wannabes.

**True HEPA filtration is non-negotiable.** Look for vacuums with genuine HEPA filters rated to capture 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns and larger — not "HEPA-style" or "HEPA-like" marketing speak. Pollen particles range from 10-200 microns, so any legitimate HEPA system will trap them, but you want the real deal for microscopic dust mites and their waste products.

**Self-emptying capability becomes essential during allergy season.** Manual emptying means you're breathing in a cloud of the exact allergens you're trying to eliminate. Self-emptying bases typically hold 30-60 days of debris, keeping allergens sealed away. The best systems use sealed bags rather than bagless chambers to prevent any escape during disposal.

**Suction power matters more for summer cleaning.** While 1,000-2,000 Pa might suffice year-round, summer dust storms and increased pollen require 2,500+ Pa of suction. Look for models with multiple suction modes so you can dial up the power when needed without killing battery life during routine cleaning.

**Smart scheduling and mapping prevent allergen buildup.** The best robot vacuums for allergies run automatically 3-4 times per week during peak season, focusing extra attention on high-traffic areas where dust accumulates. Room-specific cleaning schedules let you hit bedrooms daily while cleaning storage areas weekly.
