## What to Look For in a Package Delivery Doorbell

When shopping for a smart doorbell specifically for package notifications, **motion detection accuracy** should be your top priority. Look for models with AI-powered detection that can differentiate between delivery drivers, random passersby, and actual package drops. The best systems offer customizable motion zones so you can focus alerts on your porch area while ignoring street traffic.

**Video quality and field of view** matter enormously for package monitoring. You need clear footage to identify delivery companies, read package labels if needed, and potentially provide evidence if something goes missing. Look for at least 1080p resolution with a 160-degree or wider viewing angle to capture your entire porch area.

**Real-time notification speed** can make the difference between catching a delivery driver or missing them entirely. The fastest systems push alerts to your phone within 3-5 seconds of detecting motion. Some doorbells also offer pre-roll recording, capturing a few seconds of footage before the motion trigger, which helps you see exactly how packages were handled.

**Integration with delivery services** is increasingly important. Some smart doorbells can automatically detect Amazon, FedEx, and UPS deliveries, sending specialized package alerts with delivery company information. This feature eliminates false alarms from regular foot traffic while ensuring you never miss an actual delivery.

Package detection is handled through Arlo's Smart detection, which can differentiate between people, vehicles, and packages with impressive accuracy.
