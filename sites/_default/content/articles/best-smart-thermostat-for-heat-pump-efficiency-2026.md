## What to Look For in a Heat Pump Smart Thermostat

**Heat pump staging compatibility** is absolutely critical. Your thermostat needs to properly control both your heat pump's compressor stages and auxiliary heating elements. Look for models that support 2-stage heat pumps with 2-stage auxiliary heat – anything less will leave performance on the table. The best thermostats use sophisticated algorithms to minimize aux heat usage, which can cost 2-3x more to operate than the heat pump itself.

**Accurate outdoor temperature sensing** separates good thermostats from great ones for heat pump applications. Heat pumps lose efficiency as outdoor temperatures drop, so your thermostat needs real-time outdoor data to make smart staging decisions. Built-in outdoor sensors or weather API integration are must-haves.

**Advanced scheduling with heat pump logic** goes beyond basic programming. The smartest thermostats understand that heat pumps work best with gradual temperature changes rather than rapid setbacks. Look for features like "smart recovery" that pre-condition your home efficiently and avoid the efficiency-killing temperature swings that trigger auxiliary heat.

**Geofencing and occupancy detection** become even more valuable with heat pumps because they help maintain steady temperatures when you're home while allowing longer, more efficient recovery periods when you're away. The best models can learn your schedule and optimize accordingly.
