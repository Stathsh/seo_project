## What to Look For in Construction Site Security Cameras

**Power independence is non-negotiable.** Construction sites rarely have reliable electrical infrastructure, so look for cameras with substantial battery life (minimum 30 days) or integrated solar panels. The best options combine both, ensuring continuous operation regardless of weather or power availability.

**Cellular connectivity beats everything else.** WiFi is unreliable or non-existent on construction sites, and running ethernet cables creates vulnerability and installation headaches. Cameras with built-in 4G LTE modems connect anywhere with cell service, which covers virtually every construction site in populated areas.

**Wide coverage reduces camera count.** Construction sites are large, open areas where a single fixed camera captures maybe 20% of what you need to monitor. Pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) capabilities or ultra-wide viewing angles let one camera do the work of three or four fixed units, dramatically reducing your total system cost.

**Weatherproofing must handle construction dust.** Standard IP65 ratings aren't enough when concrete dust, metal shavings, and heavy machinery create harsh conditions. Look for IP66 or IP67 ratings with sealed housings that won't accumulate debris in moving parts.

## Arlo Pro 5S 2K

The Arlo Pro 5S 2K delivers premium construction site monitoring with exceptional 2K HDR video quality and integrated smart features that automatically distinguish between workers, vehicles, and potential threats. Its color night vision captures usable footage in complete darkness, crucial for construction sites targeted after hours.

The 160° field of view covers more area than typical security cameras, reducing the number of units needed across large construction zones. The built-in spotlight activates automatically when motion is detected, serving as both a deterrent and illumination source for better night footage. Battery life reaches 6 months with moderate usage, though construction sites with frequent activity will need more regular charging.

Smart detection features shine in construction environments. The camera differentiates between authorized personnel and unauthorized visitors, reducing false alerts from legitimate work activity. Push notifications include thumbnails showing exactly what triggered the alert, letting you quickly assess whether response is needed.

The biggest drawback is the requirement for the Arlo Smart subscription ($4.99/month per camera) to access cloud storage and advanced AI features. Without the subscription, you're limited to live viewing and basic motion detection. The camera also requires a WiFi connection or Arlo SmartHub, limiting placement options on sites without internet infrastructure.

This camera works best for smaller construction projects or sites with existing WiFi coverage where premium video quality and smart detection features justify the ongoing subscription costs.

## Ring Stick Up Cam Elite

The Ring Stick Up Cam Elite brings reliable Power-over-Ethernet connectivity to construction sites with existing network infrastructure, delivering consistent power and data transmission through a single cable connection. This eliminates battery management entirely while providing rock-solid performance.

The 1080p video quality captures clear details during daylight hours, while the 130° viewing angle covers substantial area from corner or perimeter mounting positions. Two-way audio lets supervisors communicate with workers or security personnel without phone calls. The camera integrates seamlessly with existing Ring security systems if you're already using Ring doorbells or alarms on associated office buildings.

Night vision performance is adequate for general monitoring, though not exceptional compared to newer models with color night vision. The camera requires Ring Protect subscription ($3/month or $30/year) for video storage beyond live viewing, adding to the total cost of ownership.

The POE requirement is both a strength and weakness. Sites with existing network infrastructure benefit from unlimited power and reliable connectivity, but this eliminates the camera from most active construction sites without permanent buildings. It's ideal for construction company offices, equipment yards, or building projects where temporary network installation is feasible.

Installation requires network planning and cable management, making this less suitable for frequently changing construction environments. Choose the Ring Stick Up Cam Elite when you have reliable network infrastructure and want to eliminate battery maintenance entirely.
