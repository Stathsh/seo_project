## What to Look For in a Smart Thermostat for Heat Pumps

When shopping for a smart thermostat specifically designed to work with heat pumps, you need features that go beyond basic programmable scheduling. **Heat pump compatibility** is crucial — look for thermostats that support both single-stage and dual-stage heat pumps, plus proper auxiliary heat control. Many standard thermostats struggle with heat pump efficiency because they don't understand when to engage backup heating elements.

**Advanced staging control** separates good thermostats from great ones. The best models use sophisticated algorithms to determine when your heat pump should run versus when auxiliary heat is needed. This prevents the common problem of backup heat kicking in too early, which can double your energy bills during cold snaps.

**Temperature differential settings** matter more for heat pumps than traditional HVAC systems. Heat pumps work most efficiently with smaller temperature swings, so look for thermostats that allow you to adjust the differential to 0.5°F or less. This keeps your system running in its sweet spot without constant cycling.

**Smart recovery and learning capabilities** help optimize heat pump performance over time. The best thermostats learn your home's thermal characteristics and start heating earlier to reach your desired temperature efficiently, rather than cranking up the auxiliary heat to catch up quickly.
