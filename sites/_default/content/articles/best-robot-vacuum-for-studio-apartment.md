## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Studio Apartments

When shopping for a robot vacuum in a compact living space, **size and navigation matter more than raw power**. Look for models under 3.2 inches tall that can slip under low-clearance furniture like platform beds and modern sofas. Studio apartments often have furniture pushed against walls to maximize space, so a slim profile is crucial.

**Runtime should match your square footage** — most studios range from 300-600 square feet, requiring 60-90 minutes of cleaning time. Models with 100+ minute runtimes ensure complete coverage without mid-clean charging breaks.

**Noise levels become critical in close quarters**, especially in buildings with thin walls. Target models operating under 60dB to avoid disturbing neighbors during evening cleaning sessions.

**Storage and charging station size** can't be overlooked in tiny spaces. Some docking stations are surprisingly bulky, eating up precious floor space. Measure your available wall area before committing to a model with an oversized base.
