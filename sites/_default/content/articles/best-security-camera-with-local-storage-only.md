## What to Look For in Local Storage Security Cameras

**Storage capacity and expandability** should be your first consideration. Look for cameras that support at least 128GB microSD cards, though 256GB or higher is ideal for weeks of continuous recording. Some models offer multiple storage options like USB drives or NAS integration for even more capacity.

**Video quality and compression efficiency** directly impacts how much footage you can store locally. Cameras with H.265 compression can store roughly twice as much video as older H.264 models in the same space. Aim for at least 1080p resolution, though 2K or 4K provides better detail for identifying faces and license plates.

**Power source flexibility** matters more with local-only cameras since you can't rely on cloud alerts for battery monitoring. Solar panels, long-lasting batteries, or reliable wired power ensure your camera stays operational. Battery life should exceed 3-6 months under normal use.

**Local network reliability** becomes critical when there's no cloud backup. Look for cameras with dual-band WiFi, ethernet options, or mesh network compatibility to maintain stable connections to your local storage hub or NVR system.
