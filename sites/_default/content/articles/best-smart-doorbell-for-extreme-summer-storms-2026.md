## What to Look For in a Storm-Ready Smart Doorbell

**Weather resistance rating** is non-negotiable — look for IP65 or higher certification that protects against heavy rain and wind-driven debris. IP54 ratings won't cut it when you're dealing with sideways rain and 60+ mph gusts. The difference between IP54 and IP65 is significant: IP65 can handle direct water jets from any direction, while IP54 only protects against water splashing.

**Connectivity redundancy** becomes crucial when storms knock out internet or create interference. The best doorbells offer dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4GHz and 5GHz) plus cellular backup options. During storms, 2.4GHz often performs better due to its longer range and better wall penetration, while 5GHz provides faster speeds when conditions are clear.

**Power backup systems** separate good doorbells from great ones during outages. Look for models with either rechargeable batteries that last 6+ months or hardwired options with battery backup. Some newer models can even tap into your home's backup power systems.

**Wind resistance and mounting strength** matter more than most people realize. A doorbell that works perfectly in calm weather can become a projectile in 70 mph winds. Look for reinforced mounting systems and low-profile designs that reduce wind load.

Best for homeowners who want professional-grade weather protection and don't mind the wiring requirement and subscription costs.

Perfect for renters or anyone who wants excellent storm protection without dealing with complex wiring, provided you don't mind subscription costs.
