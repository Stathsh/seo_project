The **Shark AI Ultra 2-in-1** is the best robot vacuum for hardwood floors and area rugs, combining powerful 2700Pa suction with intelligent navigation that seamlessly transitions between different floor types while automatically adjusting cleaning modes.

When you're dealing with mixed flooring, you need a robot vacuum that can handle the delicate dance between hardwood floors and area rugs without getting stuck, missing spots, or damaging your surfaces. Most robot vacuums excel at one or the other, but finding one that masters both requires careful consideration of suction power, navigation intelligence, and brush design.

## What to Look For

**Variable Suction Control** is crucial for mixed flooring. Your robot needs enough power (at least 2000Pa) to pull debris from rug fibers while being gentle enough on hardwood to avoid scattering dust. Look for models with automatic carpet detection that boost suction by 3-5x when transitioning onto rugs.

**Advanced Navigation Systems** prevent the frustrating scenario where your robot gets stuck on rug edges or repeatedly bumps into furniture. LiDAR or visual SLAM technology creates accurate maps, while edge sensors help navigate area rug transitions smoothly. Cheaper models with random navigation patterns often struggle with rug boundaries.

**Dual-Brush Systems** handle different debris types effectively. A combination of rubber extractors (for hardwood) and bristle brushes (for rugs) ensures optimal cleaning performance. Single-brush systems often excel at one floor type while underperforming on the other.

**Obstacle Management** becomes critical with area rugs. Look for models with advanced obstacle avoidance that can identify rug edges, furniture legs, and other hazards. The ability to climb thresholds of at least 15-20mm ensures smooth transitions onto thicker rugs.
