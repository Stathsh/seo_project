## What to Look For in Construction Progress Monitoring Cameras

### Extended Battery Life and Power Options
Construction projects span months, not days. Look for cameras offering at least 3-6 months of battery life on a single charge, or better yet, solar charging capabilities. The last thing you want is dead cameras during critical construction phases. Removable batteries are a major plus since you can swap them without dismounting the entire camera setup.

### Weather Resistance and Durability
Construction sites are brutal environments. Your camera needs legitimate IP65 or IP67 weatherproofing to handle rain, dust, extreme temperatures, and the occasional flying debris. Cheap cameras with basic "weather resistance" won't cut it when facing months of exposure to construction site conditions.

### High-Resolution Video for Progress Documentation
You're not just monitoring for security—you're documenting progress. 2K or 4K resolution is essential for capturing fine details like material deliveries, work quality, and project milestones. Lower resolution cameras might catch intruders, but they won't show you whether the electrical rough-in was completed properly.

### Reliable Remote Access and Cloud Storage
Spotty cellular connectivity is common on construction sites. Look for cameras with robust cloud storage plans and mobile apps that work reliably even with poor internet connections. Local storage backup via SD cards provides additional peace of mind when connectivity fails.
