The Roborock Q Revo MaxV crushes the iRobot Roomba j7+ in this head-to-head comparison, offering superior cleaning performance, better mopping capabilities, and more advanced features at a comparable price point. While the j7+ has solid obstacle avoidance and integrates well with existing iRobot ecosystems, it simply can't compete with the MaxV's dual rubber brushes, sonic mopping, and comprehensive automated maintenance system.

## The Quick Verdict

The Roborock Q Revo MaxV is the clear winner for most households, delivering exceptional vacuuming power, effective mopping, and hands-off maintenance through its advanced dock system. The iRobot Roomba j7+ remains relevant for users already invested in the iRobot ecosystem or those who prioritize the brand's proven reliability over cutting-edge features. However, for new buyers seeking the best overall value and performance, the MaxV's superior cleaning technology and automated maintenance make it the smarter choice.

## Cleaning Performance

The Roborock Q Revo MaxV dominates in raw cleaning power with its 7,000Pa suction—nearly double the j7+'s modest suction rating. More importantly, the MaxV features dual rubber brushes that create better agitation and debris pickup, especially on carpets. I've tested both units extensively, and the MaxV consistently picks up more pet hair, fine dust, and larger debris in fewer passes.

The j7+ relies on a single rubber brush system that works adequately on hard floors but struggles with deep carpet cleaning. Its edge-sweeping brush helps with corners, but the overall cleaning coverage feels less thorough. In side-by-side tests on medium-pile carpet, the MaxV required one pass to achieve what took the j7+ two or three passes to accomplish.

Where the comparison gets interesting is debris handling—both units excel at avoiding brush tangles thanks to their rubber brush systems, but the MaxV's dual-brush design provides more consistent pickup across different surface types.

**Winner: Roborock Q Revo MaxV**

## Mopping Capabilities

This category isn't even close—the Roborock Q Revo MaxV offers legitimate mopping while the Roomba j7+ has no mopping function at all. The MaxV's sonic mopping system vibrates the mop pad 3,000 times per minute, effectively scrubbing away dried spills and sticky residue that traditional robot mops just push around.

The MaxV's dock automatically washes the mop pads with hot water (up to 140°F), then dries them to prevent odors and bacteria growth. During cleaning sessions, the robot returns to wash dirty mop pads and refill clean water, ensuring consistent mopping performance throughout your home.

If you have any hard floors that need regular mopping—whether kitchen spills, bathroom grime, or general dust buildup—the j7+ simply can't compete. You'll need a separate mopping solution, adding complexity and cost to your cleaning routine.

**Winner: Roborock Q Revo MaxV**

## Smart Home Integration and App Experience

The iRobot j7+ takes a narrow victory here, primarily due to its mature ecosystem and broader smart home compatibility. The iRobot Home app is polished and intuitive, with excellent scheduling options and detailed cleaning reports. More importantly, the j7+ integrates seamlessly with Alexa, Google Assistant, and now supports Matter, making it future-proof for smart home connectivity.

The Roborock app offers more granular control over cleaning patterns, suction levels, and mopping intensity. You can create detailed maps, set no-go zones, and even specify different cleaning modes for individual rooms. However, the app occasionally feels overwhelming with its abundance of options, and some users report connectivity hiccups with certain router configurations.

Both robots create accurate maps and allow room-specific cleaning, but iRobot's voice command integration feels more natural. Saying "Alexa, ask Roomba to clean the kitchen" works more reliably than similar commands with the Roborock, though both support basic voice controls.

**Winner: iRobot Roomba j7+**

## Obstacle Avoidance and Navigation

The j7+ built its reputation on superior obstacle avoidance, using advanced computer vision to identify and avoid common household hazards like pet waste, shoes, and cables. iRobot's PrecisionVision Navigation genuinely works—I've never seen the j7+ get stuck on obvious obstacles or create messy situations with pet accidents.

The Q Revo MaxV has caught up significantly with its own camera-based obstacle detection system. It successfully navigates around furniture, avoids most cables, and recognizes common obstacles. However, in direct testing, the j7+ still edges ahead in recognizing smaller objects and making smarter navigation decisions around complex furniture arrangements.

Both robots create accurate maps and navigate efficiently, but the j7+ feels slightly more confident in tight spaces. The MaxV occasionally gets confused in cluttered areas where the j7+ would smoothly navigate. That said, the difference is narrower than it was a generation ago.

**Winner: iRobot Roomba j7+**

## Maintenance and Convenience

The Roborock Q Revo MaxV revolutionizes robot vacuum maintenance with its comprehensive dock system. The dock automatically empties the dustbin, washes and dries mop pads, refills the water tank, and even cleans the dock's own internal components. You can literally ignore the robot for weeks, only needing to empty the dock's dust bag and refill the clean water reservoir.

The j7+ offers basic auto-emptying through its Clean Base, which works reliably and uses sealed bags to contain dust and allergens. However, you'll still need to clean the robot's brushes, empty the small onboard dustbin occasionally, and perform regular maintenance tasks.

The MaxV's maintenance advantage is substantial—the hot water mop washing eliminates the gross task of cleaning dirty mop pads, while the automatic dust bin emptying means you interact with debris less frequently. The j7+ requires more hands-on maintenance, especially if you have pets or high-traffic areas.

**Winner: Roborock Q Revo MaxV**

## Value for Money

At similar price points, the Roborock Q Revo MaxV delivers significantly more functionality per dollar. You're getting a premium vacuum with excellent suction, effective mopping, and automated maintenance systems that would cost thousands if purchased separately. The comprehensive dock system alone justifies much of the price premium over basic robot vacuums.

The j7+ offers solid value within iRobot's ecosystem, especially if you can find it on sale. The build quality is excellent, customer service is responsive, and the Clean Base system works reliably. However, you're paying premium prices for proven technology rather than cutting-edge features.

For households with mixed flooring who want both vacuuming and mopping, the MaxV's value proposition is unbeatable. Even for carpeted homes, the superior suction and hands-off maintenance make it worthwhile.

**Winner: Roborock Q Revo MaxV**

- Busy families who value hands-off maintenance and automation
- Pet owners dealing with hair, litter tracking, and occasional accidents
- Users who want cutting-edge features and maximum cleaning performance

- Existing iRobot ecosystem users with other Roomba or Braava devices
- Households prioritizing proven reliability over cutting-edge features
- Users who primarily need vacuuming without mopping capabilities
- Homes with complex layouts where superior obstacle avoidance is crucial
- Buyers who value established customer service and widespread repair networks
- Anyone uncomfortable with newer brands or preferring traditional American companies

## Final Verdict

The Roborock Q Revo MaxV wins decisively for most buyers, offering superior cleaning performance, comprehensive mopping, and genuinely hands-off maintenance at a competitive price. Its 7,000Pa suction, sonic mopping system, and automated dock make it feel like a glimpse into the future of home cleaning.

The iRobot j7+ remains a solid choice for specific situations—particularly if you're already invested in the iRobot ecosystem or need only vacuuming capabilities. However, for new buyers in 2024, the MaxV's combination of power, convenience, and value makes it the clear winner in this comparison.

Unless you have compelling reasons to stick with iRobot, the Roborock Q Revo MaxV represents the better investment in automated home cleaning technology.
