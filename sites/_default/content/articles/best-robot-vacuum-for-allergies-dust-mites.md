## What to Look For in a Robot Vacuum for Allergies and Dust Mites

When shopping for an allergy-friendly robot vacuum, **HEPA filtration is non-negotiable**. True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns and larger, which includes dust mite debris, pet dander, and pollen. Look for models with sealed filtration systems that prevent particles from escaping back into your air.

**Suction power matters more than marketing numbers suggest**. While manufacturers love to tout "X times more suction," what really counts is consistent airflow and brush design. Dual rubber brushes tend to work better than bristle brushes for fine particles, as they create better contact with surfaces and don't scatter debris.

**Self-emptying bases are game-changers for allergy sufferers**. Manual emptying exposes you to concentrated allergens every few cleaning cycles. Self-emptying models transfer debris into sealed bags or bins, keeping you away from the mess for weeks or even months.

**Carpet deep-cleaning capability separates good from great**. Dust mites live deep in carpet fibers, so you need a robot that can agitate and extract embedded particles, not just surface debris. Look for models with carpet boost modes and brush systems designed for deep cleaning.
