## What to Look For in Starlight Night Vision Cameras

When shopping for the best smart security camera with starlight night vision, focus on these critical specs that actually determine low-light performance:

**Sensor size and aperture** are everything. Look for cameras with f/1.6 or wider apertures and larger sensors (1/2.8" minimum). Marketing terms like "starlight" are meaningless without the hardware to back it up — a tiny f/2.4 aperture will struggle regardless of fancy algorithms.

**Color vs. infrared switching** should be intelligent and customizable. The best cameras automatically switch to infrared when color footage becomes too grainy, but you want manual control over this threshold. Some situations benefit from color footage even if it's slightly noisy.

**Smart detection accuracy in low light** separates premium cameras from budget options. Advanced AI that can distinguish between people, vehicles, and animals when there's barely any light requires serious processing power and well-trained algorithms.

**Recording and storage flexibility** becomes crucial for security cameras since you'll likely capture more footage during darker evening hours. Cloud storage costs add up quickly, so prioritize cameras with reliable local storage options and reasonable cloud pricing.
