## What to Look For in a Pool Area Robot Vacuum

### Water and Moisture Resistance
Pool areas are inherently wet environments. Look for robots with IPX4 rating or higher that can handle splashes, pool deck dampness, and morning dew without suffering damage. Standard indoor robot vacuums will fail quickly in these conditions — you need models specifically designed for outdoor moisture exposure.

### Powerful Suction for Outdoor Debris
Pool areas accumulate heavier debris than indoor spaces — wet leaves, sand, grass clippings, and sunscreen bottles. Seek vacuums with at least 2000Pa suction power and brush systems designed for outdoor debris. Dual-brush systems work best, combining rubber and bristle brushes to handle both fine particles and larger items.

### All-Terrain Navigation
Pool decks feature various surfaces — textiles, concrete, stone pavers, and transitions between dry and wet areas. Advanced mapping technology with multi-surface sensors ensures thorough cleaning across different textures and elevation changes common around pools.

### Large Dustbin and Easy Maintenance
Outdoor cleaning means more debris volume. Look for dustbins over 500ml capacity and easy-empty designs that don't require you to handle dirty filters with wet hands. Quick-release mechanisms are essential when you're dealing with pool chemicals on your hands.
